---
title: Home screen & folders
description: How to organize tournaments into folders, sort your list, and duplicate, move, or export items in Poker Club HQ.
---

The home screen lists everything you've created — tournaments, folders, and chip sets. Folders are how you group tournaments into a season or league, and they're what [Club stats](/info/club-stats/) runs on.

## Adding something new

Tap **＋** in the top right to open **Add new**:

- **Folder** — A container for tournaments and other folders
- **Tournament** — A new tournament ([setting one up](/info/setup/))
- **Import from file** — Load a tournament, chip set, or folder that was exported from another device

An empty home screen prompts you with *"Tap the ＋ in the top right to add a tournament."*

## Folders

Tap a folder to open it. Everything inside — including the **＋** button and the sort header — works the same way as the home screen, so you can nest folders inside folders up to four levels deep. Once you're that deep, **Folder** stops appearing in the **Add new** sheet.

To rename a folder, open it and tap its name in the app bar.

:::tip
Put each season or league in its own folder. The **Club stats** button appears in a folder's app bar as soon as that folder holds a tournament, and it covers every tournament in the folder *and its subfolders* — but it's deliberately unavailable on the home screen itself, so a season kept loose at the top level can't be totalled.
:::

## Sorting the list

Once a list has two or more items, a header appears with two columns:

- **Name** — Sort alphabetically
- **Modified** — Sort by when each item last changed

Tap either one to sort by it; tap the same one again to reverse the direction. The choice applies to every folder, not just the one you're in.

## Item menu

Tap **⋮** on any row for:

- **Duplicate** — Make a copy. Duplicating a folder copies everything inside it.
- **Export** — Share the item as a `.pchq` file. Exporting a folder bundles the whole tree into one file.
- **Download** — Save that file directly (on the web)
- **Move to...** — Move the item into a different folder. This only appears once you have at least one folder to move it to.
- **Move to trash** — See [Trash & restore](/info/trash/)

### Opening a file someone sent you

On an iPhone or iPad, tap the `.pchq` file wherever it arrives — a Mail attachment, the Files app, an AirDrop — and it opens in Poker Club HQ and lands on your home screen. Tournament files from the old Texas Holdem Poker Timer app (`.ptt`) open the same way.

On Android and the web, use **Import from file** in the **＋** menu instead.

## Deleting

Swipe an item left to move it to the trash. Nothing is erased right away — a trash button appears at the bottom of the screen whenever the trash has anything in it, and you can restore from there. See [Trash & restore](/info/trash/).
