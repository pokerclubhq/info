Club stats is a season dashboard for a folder of tournaments. While [Stats & reporting](/info/stats/) covers a single tournament, Club stats aggregates every completed tournament in a folder — subfolders included — into a league-style view: leaderboard, points race, money, and attendance.

## Opening Club stats

Organize a season's tournaments into a folder, then tap the leaderboard button in the folder's top bar. The button appears once the folder contains at least one completed tournament.

<Image src={clubStats00001} alt="A season folder of monthly tournaments with the Club stats leaderboard button in the top bar" width={720} />

:::note
Only completed tournaments count toward Club stats — a tournament joins the dashboard once it has finished. Tournaments in subfolders are included too.
:::

## Leaderboard

Players ranked by season points, along with the number of games each has played. Points come from the same customizable formula used on each tournament's [Stats screen](/info/stats/#points-formula), summed across the season. If one person shows up as two names, merge them in the [Player Directory](/info/player-directory/) and their rows combine.

<Image src={clubStats00002} alt="Club stats Leaderboard tab ranking players by games played and season points" width={720} />

## Points

The points race over time: each of the top 10 players' cumulative points, tournament by tournament, across the season.

<Image src={clubStats00003} alt="Club stats Points tab charting the top 10 players' cumulative points across the season" width={720} />

## Money

The money view per player: total **winnings**, total **buy-ins** (including add-ons and rebuys), the **net** of the two, and **ROI** — return on investment.

<Image src={clubStats00004} alt="Club stats Money tab listing each player's winnings, buy-ins, net, and ROI" width={720} />

## Attendance

Season participation and performance: **games** played, **wins**, **average finish**, **final tables** made, and **knockouts** dealt.

<Image src={clubStats00005} alt="Club stats Attendance tab listing games, wins, average finish, final tables, and knockouts per player" width={720} />

## Exporting

Use the share and download buttons in the top bar to export the current tab as a CSV file — handy for spreadsheets or posting season standings to your club.

:::tip[No season yet?]
Use [Simulate](/info/simulate/) with more than one tournament to generate a complete sample season, then open its folder's Club stats to explore the dashboard.
:::
