Club stats is a season dashboard for a folder of tournaments. While [Stats & reporting](/info/stats/) covers a single tournament, Club stats aggregates every completed tournament in a folder — subfolders included — into a league-style view: leaderboard, points race, money, and attendance.

## Opening Club stats

Organize a season's tournaments into a [folder](/info/folders/), then tap the leaderboard button in the folder's top bar.

<Image src={clubStats00001} alt="A season folder of monthly tournaments with the Club stats leaderboard button in the top bar" width={720} />

The button shows up as soon as the folder holds at least one tournament, finished or not. It isn't offered on the top-level home list, so a season needs its own folder.

:::note
Only completed tournaments count toward Club stats — a tournament joins the dashboard once it has finished. Tournaments in subfolders are included too. Until one has finished, the dashboard reads "No completed tournaments yet."
:::

## Leaderboard

Who's winning the season. Columns are **Rank**, **Name**, **Games** played, and **Points** — points coming from the same customizable formula used on each tournament's [Stats screen](/info/stats/#points-formula), summed across the season.

Tap a player to open their season game by game: every tournament they played, where they finished, and the points it earned them.

If one person shows up as two names, merge them in the [Player Directory](/info/player-directory/) and their rows combine.

<Image src={clubStats00002} alt="Club stats Leaderboard tab ranking players by games played and season points" width={720} />

## Points

The points race over time, for when the standings are close: each of the top 10 players' cumulative points, tournament by tournament, across the season.

<Image src={clubStats00003} alt="Club stats Points tab charting the top 10 players' cumulative points across the season" width={720} />

## Money

Who's actually ahead: **total winnings** (including bounty money), total **buy-ins** (including add-ons, rebuys, and bounty chips), the **net** of the two, and **ROI** — return on investment.

<Image src={clubStats00004} alt="Club stats Money tab listing each player's winnings, buy-ins, net, and ROI" width={720} />

## Attendance

Season participation and consistency: **games** played, **wins**, **average finish**, **final tables** (top-9 finishes), and **knockouts** dealt. Average finish counts only the games where a player got a finishing place, so someone removed mid-tournament isn't penalized for it.

<Image src={clubStats00005} alt="Club stats Attendance tab listing games, wins, average finish, final tables, and knockouts per player" width={720} />

## Exporting

Use the share and download buttons in the top bar to export the season as a CSV file — handy for spreadsheets or posting standings to your club.

The export is the whole season in one sheet, not just the tab you're looking at: each player's games, wins, average finish, final tables, knockouts, winnings, buy-ins, net, and total points, followed by one column per tournament showing the points they earned in it.

:::tip[No season yet?]
Use [Simulate](/info/simulate/) with more than one tournament to generate a complete sample season, then open its folder's Club stats to explore the dashboard.
:::
