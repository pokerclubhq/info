Club stats is a season dashboard for a folder of tournaments. While [Stats & reporting](/info/stats/) covers a single tournament, Club stats aggregates every completed tournament in a folder — subfolders included — into a league-style view: leaderboard, points race, money, and attendance.

## Opening Club stats

Organize a season's tournaments into a [folder](/info/folders/), then tap the leaderboard button in the folder's top bar.

<Image src={clubStats00001} alt="A season folder of monthly tournaments with the Club stats leaderboard button in the top bar" width={720} />

The button shows up as soon as the folder holds at least one tournament, finished or not. It isn't offered on the top-level home list, so a season needs its own folder.

:::note
Only completed tournaments count toward Club stats — a tournament joins the dashboard once it has finished. Tournaments in subfolders are included too. Until one has finished, the dashboard reads "No completed tournaments yet."
:::

## Leaderboard

Who's winning the season. Columns are **Rank**, **Name**, **Games** played, **Points**, and **Average points** — points coming from the same customizable formula used on each tournament's [Stats screen](/info/stats/#points-formula), summed across the season. Average points divides a player's total by the games they played, so someone who missed a few nights can be compared fairly with someone who came to every one.

Tap a player to open their season game by game: every tournament they played, where they finished, and the points it earned them.

If one person shows up as two names, merge them in the [Player Directory](/info/player-directory/) and their rows combine.

To show the leaderboard on the timer screen during a game, add the **Leaderboard** [widget](/info/widgets/#leaderboard). Scripts can read the same numbers from [`clubStats`](/info/scripting-data/#club-stats).

The widget works on a [shared tournament](/info/qr-sharing/) too: viewers see your season's standings without a plan of their own. Your app sends the standings whenever it has a tournament from the folder open, so they stay up to date as long as you run the game yourself.

## Points

The points race over time, for when the standings are close: each of the top 10 players' cumulative points, tournament by tournament, across the season.

<Image src={clubStats00003} alt="Club stats Points tab charting the top 10 players' cumulative points across the season" width={720} />

## Money

Who's actually ahead: **total winnings** (including bounty money), total **buy-ins** (including add-ons, rebuys, and bounty chips), the **net** of the two, and **ROI** — return on investment.

<Image src={clubStats00004} alt="Club stats Money tab listing each player's winnings, buy-ins, net, and ROI" width={720} />

## Attendance

Season participation and consistency: **games** played, **wins**, **average finish**, **final tables** (top-9 finishes), **ITM** (in-the-money finishes, the games where a player won part of the prize pool; bounty money alone doesn't count), and **knockouts** dealt. Average finish counts only the games where a player got a finishing place, so someone removed mid-tournament isn't penalized for it.

<Image src={clubStats00005} alt="Club stats Attendance tab listing games, wins, average finish, final tables, and knockouts per player" width={720} />

## Game order

The points race, each player's game-by-game list, the export's tournament columns, and the standings in a reopened game follow the order the games were played. Club stats takes that order from the tournaments' titles when it can:

- A copy made with **Save a copy, then reset** ends with the date its game finished, as in "Friday Game 26-10-03". Copies go by that date, and the tournament they were copied from goes by the day it finished, always after its copies.
- When every title starts with a different number, as in "01. Game day" and "02. Game day", the games go by that number. A date at the start, as in "03.10. Game day", isn't such a number, but a day before a month's name is: "7 November" would come before "17 October". Write such dates in numbers, as in "17.10." or "10/17", or after the name.

Otherwise the games go by when each one's last player was knocked out. Undoing that knockout and entering it again on a later day moves the game to the end of the season, so if you correct finished games, number them.

## Exporting

Use the share and download buttons in the top bar to export the season as a CSV file — handy for spreadsheets or posting standings to your club.

The export is the whole season in one sheet, not just the tab you're looking at: each player's games, wins, average finish, final tables, ITM finishes, knockouts, winnings, buy-ins, net, total points, and average points, followed by one column per tournament showing the points they earned in it.
