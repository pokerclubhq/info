title: Tournament log

---

The Log screen keeps a detailed, timestamped record of everything that happens in your tournament.

## What gets logged

Every significant event is automatically recorded:

- **Timer events** — Timer started, paused, and total play time
- **Level changes** — Blind increases and break starts, including the new blind values and duration
- **Buy-ins** — Player name, chip amount, and cost
- **Addons** — Player name, chips added, and cost
- **Rebuys / Reentries** — Player name, new chip amount, and cost
- **Knockouts** — Who was eliminated and by whom
- **Removals** — Players who left the game
- **Seating changes** — Table and seat assignments
- **Bounty chips** — Bounty chip purchases with player names and costs

## Viewing the log

Entries are displayed in reverse chronological order (newest first), grouped by timestamp. Each entry shows:

- **Icon** — A type-specific icon (timer, cards, person, etc.)
- **Title** — Description of the event (e.g., "John eliminated by Sally")
- **Subtitle** — Additional details like chip and cost amounts

## Undo and redo

Player-related log entries (buy-ins, addons, rebuys, knockouts, removals, seating, bounty chip purchases) can be undone and redone.

- **Undo** — Tap the undo button in the app bar to reverse the most recent player action. The entry stays in the log but is marked as disabled (shown with reduced opacity).
- **Redo** — Tap the redo button to restore a previously undone entry.

These buttons only appear when there are actions available to undo or redo.

:::note
Undo and redo are also available from the Players screen, so you can reverse actions without switching to the Log.
:::

## Replay

When the timer is paused and the log has entries, a "Replay" floating action button appears at the bottom of the Log screen. Tap it to open the Replay view. See [Replay](/info/replay/) for details on stepping through your tournament visually.
