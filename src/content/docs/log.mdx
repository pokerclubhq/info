---
title: Tournament log
description: How to view, edit, and undo tournament events in Poker Club HQ.
---

The Log screen keeps a detailed, timestamped record of everything that happens in your tournament. It's more than a record: the log *is* the tournament. Seating, payouts, and stats are all derived from these entries, which is why undoing or editing one ripples through the rest of the app.

## What gets logged

Every significant event is automatically recorded:

- **Timer events** — Timer started and timer paused
- **Level changes** — Blind increases and break starts, including the new blind values and duration
- **Money and chips** — Buy-ins, addons, rebuys, and bounty chip purchases, each with the player, chip amount, and cost
- **Knockouts** — Who was eliminated and by whom
- **Removals** — Players who left the game
- **Seating changes** — Table and seat assignments
- **Player chips / Player hands** — A recorded chip count, or a player's hole cards. These arrive from a connected player's self-report (see [Player profile updates](/info/player-updates/)), from **Add log entry** below, or from [Simulate](/info/simulate/), and they're what feed the chip leader and high hand timer widgets

## Viewing the log

Entries run in chronological order with the newest at the bottom, and the screen opens scrolled to the latest event. A timestamp pill heads each new minute. Each entry shows:

- **Icon** — A type-specific icon (timer, cards, person, etc.)
- **Title** — Description of the event (e.g., "John eliminated by Sally")
- **Subtitle** — Additional details like chip and cost amounts

## Undo and redo

Player entries (buy-ins, addons, rebuys, knockouts, removals, seating, bounty chip purchases) and Player chips / Player hands entries can be undone and redone.

- **Undo** — Tap the undo button in the app bar to reverse the most recent action. The entry stays in the log but is marked as disabled (shown with reduced opacity).
- **Redo** — Tap the redo button to restore a previously undone entry.

These buttons only appear when there are actions available to undo or redo. An entry flagged as invalid can't be undone — editing is how you fix those.

:::note
Undo and redo are also available from the Players screen, where they show you the affected entry for confirmation first.
:::

## Working with a single entry

Tap the **⋮** button on a row to act on that one entry:

- **Undo / Redo** — The same reversal as the app bar buttons, but aimed at this entry
- **Edit** — Change the players or values on a player or a Player chips / Player hands entry
- **Delete** — Remove the entry outright. Available to the tournament's owner, and to whoever created the entry

A **⚠** in place of the **⋮** means the entry no longer fits the rest of the log — a buy-in for a player who was already knocked out, say. Tap it and edit the entry to sort it out.

## The log menu

The app bar's **⋮** menu holds three more actions:

- **Add log entry** — Record something by hand. Choose the entry type (Player chips or Player hands, plus any custom types the tournament defines), pick the players, and enter their values.
- **Replay** — Step through the tournament visually; see below.
- **Reset** — Return the tournament to its unplayed state. If it has already finished, you're offered the chance to save a completed copy first.

## Replay

When the timer is paused and the log has entries, the app bar's **⋮** menu offers **Replay**. Tap it to open the Replay view. See [Replay](/info/replay/) for details on stepping through your tournament visually.

Replay isn't offered for tournaments using simplified player data.
