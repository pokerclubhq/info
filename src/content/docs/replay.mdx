title: Replay

The Replay screen lets you step through your entire tournament event by event, watching how players were seated, moved, and eliminated over time. It's the quickest way to check whether a balancing move was fair, or to find the point where table balance went wrong.

## Opening replay

When the timer is paused and the log has entries, the [Log screen](/info/log/)'s app bar **⋮** menu offers **Replay**. Replay isn't offered for tournaments using simplified player data.

## Replay controls

The replay screen has a table visualization at the top and a scrollable event list at the bottom. Five playback controls sit between them:

- **Restart** — Jump back to the very first log entry
- **Previous** — Step back one moment
- **Play / Pause** — Auto-advance through the tournament at the selected speed
- **Next** — Step forward one moment
- **Speed selector** — Choose playback speed: 1x, 2x, 4x, or 8x

Playback moves a moment at a time rather than an entry at a time: events that happened together — a knockout and the reseating it triggered, say — advance as a unit, so the tables are never drawn mid-change. Your chosen speed is remembered, as is where you left off in each tournament.

## Table visualization

As you step through events, the table display updates in real time to show:

- Which tables are active
- Where each player is seated, by name or photo
- Eliminations and seating changes

Animated chip particles fly across the table on each elimination. Animations are enabled for tournaments with up to 1,000 players.

## Event picker

The scrollable picker at the bottom shows each log entry in order. Scroll to jump directly to any point in the tournament. Only enabled entries are shown — entries that have been undone are skipped.
