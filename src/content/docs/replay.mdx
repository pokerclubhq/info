title: Replay

The Replay screen lets you step through your entire tournament event by event, watching how players were seated, moved, and eliminated over time.

## Opening replay

From the Log screen, tap the "Replay" floating action button. This button appears when the timer is paused and the log contains entries.

## Replay controls

The replay screen has a table visualization at the top and a scrollable event list at the bottom. Five playback controls sit between them:

- **Restart** — Jump back to the very first log entry
- **Previous** — Step back one entry
- **Play / Pause** — Auto-advance through entries at the selected speed
- **Next** — Step forward one entry
- **Speed selector** — Choose playback speed: 1x, 2x, 4x, or 8x

## Table visualization

As you step through events, the table display updates in real time to show:

- Which tables are active
- Where each player is seated
- Player chip counts
- Eliminations and seating changes

Animated chip particles provide visual flair as the tournament progresses. Animations are enabled for tournaments with up to 1,000 players.

## Event picker

The scrollable picker at the bottom shows each log entry in order. Scroll to jump directly to any point in the tournament. Only enabled entries are shown — entries that have been undone are skipped.

## Use cases

Replay is useful for:

- **Reviewing decisions** — Step through seating and balancing moves to evaluate fairness
- **Spotting issues** — Find where table balance went wrong or where a player was moved too many times
- **Entertainment** — Watch the tournament unfold after it's over, or show it on a projector during the awards
