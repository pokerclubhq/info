Need a hand running a tournament — someone working the players screen while you watch the clock, or a co-host on a second device? Give them a **role** in your [Player Directory](/info/player-directory/) and they can help run your tournaments from their own device, with every change syncing between you in real time.

This is different from [QR code sharing](/info/qr-sharing/), which gives anyone a read-only view without an account. Use roles when someone needs to *make changes*; use QR sharing when they just need to *watch*.

:::note
A role belongs to the *player*, not to one tournament. Someone you make a Table Captain is a Table Captain in every tournament of yours they connect to, until you change it back.
:::

## The three roles

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Player** | The default. No edit access — they get the same read-only timer any QR viewer sees. |
| **Table Captain** | Runs the game at the table: buy-ins, addons, rebuys, reentries, knockouts, seating, and chip counts and hands. |
| **Director** | Full control, the same as you. |

### What a Table Captain sees

A Table Captain gets four screens — **Timer**, **Players**, **Tables**, and **Log** — and nothing else in the navigation drawer.

<Image src={captainDrawer} alt="A Table Captain's navigation drawer showing only Timer screen, Players, Tables, and Log" width={320} />

They can do everything on the Players, Tables, and Log screens that keeps the game moving: enter buy-ins, addons, rebuys and reentries, record knockouts, seat players and move them between tables and seats, and record chip counts and hands.

What they *can't* touch:

- **The clock.** No play/pause, no bottom bar, no jumping to another level, no resetting the tournament. The drawer's mini timer shows them the time and blinds, without the controls that change them.
- **Settings, Blinds, Payouts, Stats, and Alerts.** Those screens aren't in their drawer.
- **Customizing the timer display**, importing customizations, or **Simulate**.
- **Deleting players** from the tournament. Every other player action is available to them.

:::note
If the tournament uses **EZ player data** (no player list), a Table Captain gets the timer screen only — there are no Players, Tables, or Log screens to hand over.
:::

### What a Director sees

Everything you see. A Director can start and pause the timer, edit the blind structure, change settings and payouts, and delete players — the full app.

Two things stay with you as the owner: **assigning roles**, and **deleting the tournament**.

## Assigning a role

1. Open the main menu and tap **Player Directory**.
2. Tap the player.
3. Tap the **role** row — it reads **Player** until you change it.

   <Image src={roleRow} alt="A player's details screen with the role row showing Table Captain and its description" width={320} />

4. Pick **Table Captain** or **Director** and tap **Save**.

   <Image src={rolePicker} alt="The role picker with Player, Table Captain, and Director options and a description under each" width={320} />

To take a role away, come back and set them to **Player**.

:::tip
The tournament **Settings** screen has a **Roles** button that explains this and jumps straight to the Player Directory.

<Image src={rolesInfo} alt="The Roles dialog opened from tournament Settings, with a Not now and an Open Player Directory button" width={320} />
:::

## What has to be true for it to work

A role is matched to a real person by **email address**. Three things have to line up:

- **The player has an email address in your Player Directory.** Add it from their details screen — tap **+** and choose **Email**. Assign a role to someone without one and the app tells you the role won't take effect yet.
- **They sign into Poker Club HQ with that same address.** Capitalization doesn't matter. A different address — a work one, say — won't match.
- **Their address is verified.** Google and Apple sign-ins are verified automatically; accounts registered with a password need one extra step (see below).

Get one of these wrong and there's no error — they simply land on the read-only viewer.

## For the helper: your email address

Everything in this section is for the person being given a role.

Your address is on your Profile screen, and you can copy it straight from there:

1. Open the navigation drawer and tap **Profile**.
2. Tap the email address (or the copy icon beside it) to copy it to the clipboard.
3. Paste it into a text or email and send it to the tournament owner, so they can put it on your player in their directory.

<Image src={profileNotVerified} alt="Profile screen showing the email address with a copy icon, an Email is not verified warning, and a Send verification email button" width={320} />

If you signed in with **Google** or **Apple**, your address is already verified — you're done here.

If you registered with **email and password**, your account starts out unverified, and the Profile screen shows an orange **Email is not verified** warning with a **Send verification email** button (above). Editing shared tournaments won't work until you clear it:

1. Tap **Send verification email**.
2. Open the email Poker Club HQ sends you and tap the link in it.
3. Come back to the app and open **Profile** again.

If your edits still don't stick a few minutes later, sign out and back in to refresh your credentials.

## Connecting to a shared tournament

A role decides *what* someone can do; they still need a link to the tournament itself. Two ways to get one:

- **Scan the QR code.** Point your camera at the QR code on the owner's timer screen and tap the link that appears.
- **Tap a link they sent you.** The owner can tap the QR code on their timer to copy the link, then send it to you in a text or email.

Either way, the app opens the tournament with whatever access your role gives you. If the owner hasn't put a QR code on their timer yet, they'll need to create one first — see [QR code sharing](/info/qr-sharing/).

:::tip
Role changes take effect live. Promote someone mid-tournament and their screens update where they stand — no reconnecting, no rescanning.
:::

:::note
If you open the link and get a read-only timer instead of edit access, one of three things is off: you aren't a player in the owner's directory with your address on you, your role is still **Player**, or your email isn't verified yet.
:::

## Roles in bulk

The directory's CSV export and import both carry a **Role** column, holding `Table Captain` or `Director` — blank means Player. Handy for setting up a room full of captains at once, or for keeping a record of who has access. See [Import from CSV file](/info/player-directory/#import-from-csv-file).

On import, roles follow the same fill-empty rule as everything else: a player who already has a role keeps it, and the file can never take one away.
