A bounty chip is a token a player buys at the start of, or during, a tournament. When the holder is knocked out, the bounty transfers to the player who eliminated them. Bounties add a "head-hunting" layer on top of the regular prize pool.

<Image src={bounties} alt="Bounty chip tracking" width={300} />

## Bounties and the prize pool

:::tip[Bounties are kept separate from the prize pool.]
The bounty chip cost is **in addition to** the buy-in cost — it is not part of it. Money paid for bounty chips never enters the prize pool shown on the [Payouts](/info/payouts/) screen. It moves directly between players as bounties are won.
:::

For example, with a $20 buy-in and a $5 bounty chip cost:

- A player who buys in and takes a bounty chip pays **$25 total** ($20 to the prize pool, $5 to the bounty pool).
- A player who buys in without a bounty chip pays **$20** (all of it to the prize pool).

## Enabling bounties

Bounties require **pro** player management. In the tournament's Settings screen, set **Player management** to `pro` at the top — the bounty settings don't appear in `basic` mode.

Then turn on the **Bounties** switch. Two settings appear:

### Bounty chip cost
The price a player pays for a single bounty chip. This is charged in addition to the buy-in cost — it is not deducted from it. Enter the dollar amount your club uses for its head-hunter pool.

### Need chip to win chip
Controls who is eligible to win a bounty when they knock out a bounty-holder.

- **On** — Only eliminators who already hold a bounty chip can win one. If a player without a bounty chip eliminates a bounty-holder, the bounty stays with the eliminated player — it isn't lost, and it still counts toward their **Total winnings**.
- **Off** — Any eliminator can win a bounty, whether they hold one themselves or not.

## Buying a bounty chip

Players can pick up a bounty chip two ways:

1. **At buy-in or reentry** — In the buy-in dialog, toggle **Buy bounty chip** before confirming. The player is charged the buy-in plus the bounty chip cost in a single transaction (logged as two separate entries).
2. **Later, during the tournament** — On the [Players](/info/players/) screen, tap an active player who doesn't already hold a bounty chip, then choose **Buy bounty chip**. The player is charged the bounty chip cost only.

A player can hold at most one bounty chip at a time, and that chip is always worth the bounty chip cost. It does **not** grow as they collect bounties — see below.

## Knockouts and bounty transfers

When you record a knockout on the Players screen, you select the eliminator (or eliminators, if multiple players contributed to the knockout). If the eliminated player held a bounty chip:

- The bounty value goes to the eliminator(s).
- If there are multiple eliminators, the value is **split equally** among them.
- If **Need chip to win chip** is on, only eliminators who already hold a bounty are eligible to share in the transfer. If none of them qualify, the bounty stays with the eliminated player.

:::note[Bounties don't snowball.]
Winning a bounty doesn't make the eliminator's own chip more valuable. Their bounty chip stays worth the bounty chip cost, and what they won is banked separately as **bounty winnings**. So when they're eventually knocked out, only one bounty's worth changes hands — not everything they collected along the way.
:::

## Where bounty data appears

Bounty activity surfaces in several places:

- **[Players](/info/players/) screen** — Each player tile shows the player's current bounty chip value and total bounty winnings.
- **[Tournament log](/info/log/)** — Bounty chip purchases are recorded as separate log entries with player name and cost. They can be undone and redone like any player action.
- **[Stats & reporting](/info/stats/)** — Four dedicated columns:
  - **Bounty held** — Current bounty chip value the player is carrying.
  - **Bounty cost** — Total the player has paid for bounty chips (separate from **Chips cost**, which is the prize-pool side).
  - **Bounty winnings** — Total bounty value the player has won by knocking out other bounty-holders.
  - **Total winnings** — Payout plus bounty money in one reporting total (the pools themselves stay separate).
- **Payouts timer screen** — A **Bounties** widget appears beside the payouts, listing the top 10 bounty winners and their winnings. It's also available from the timer's widget gallery, so you can place it on any screen.
