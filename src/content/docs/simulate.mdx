Simulate instantly plays out the rest of a tournament — every hand, knockout, rebuy, add-on, and payout — producing a realistically completed tournament in seconds. It's great for exploring the app's features, seeing how a blind structure plays out, or generating a whole season of sample data for [Club stats](/info/club-stats/).

## Where to find it

For a tournament that hasn't been started yet, a **Simulate** button sits in the lower-right corner of the timer screen:

<Image src={simulate00001} alt="Timer screen of an unstarted tournament with the Simulate button in the lower right" width={320} />

Simulate is also in the tournament menu — the sparkle button in the top row. It's there whenever the timer is paused and the tournament isn't already over, so it works on a game that's partway done: the simulation picks up from wherever the tournament currently stands and plays it to completion.

<Image src={simulate00002} alt="Tournament menu with the Simulate sparkle button in its top row" width={320} />

:::note
If the tournament has no players yet, Simulate fills the seats itself — using the expected player count from the tournament's settings, or asking how many to add when that isn't set (up to 100). It drafts them from your [Player Directory](/info/player-directory/) and invents sample players if it needs more.
:::

## Simulation options

Tapping Simulate opens the **Simulate tournament** sheet:

<Image src={simulate00003} alt="Simulate tournament sheet showing the number of tournaments slider and chip count recording options" width={320} />

### Number of tournaments

Leave the slider at **1** to simulate just this tournament to completion. Choose more than one to create a whole [season](#simulating-a-season) of up to 12 tournaments.

### Record chip counts

Choose how much chip-count detail the simulation writes to the log:

- **Don't record** — No chip counts are saved.
- **Before each chop** — When two or more players bust in the same hand, saves what they held going in, ready for chip and ICM chops.
- **At every break** — Snapshots all stacks when the clock goes on break.
- **After every hand** — Saves the new stacks of players who posted, bet, called, or won.

### Record player hands

Choose which hole cards make it into the log:

- **Don't record** — No hands are saved.
- **High hands only** — Saves a hand only when it beats the best one shown so far.
- **Knockouts only** — Saves showdown cards only on the hand that busts a player.
- **Every showdown** — Saves the cards of everyone who reaches a showdown.

<Image src={simulate00004} alt="Simulate tournament sheet showing the player hand recording options and the Simulate button" width={320} />

## Simulating a season

Choosing more than one tournament creates a season in a new [folder](/info/folders/) next to the tournament, named for its date range — for example "Aug 2025 - Jul 2026".

Inside are completed copies of the tournament, one per month going back. Each falls on the same weekday-of-the-month as today (if today is the third Friday, so is every earlier tournament) and starts at noon. The last copy picks up from the tournament's current state and ends around now. The tournament itself is left unchanged, and the app opens the new folder when the season finishes.

The season is built to look like a real league: each player keeps a consistent skill level from month to month, and a random 75–100% of the roster turns up to each tournament, so attendance and results vary the way they would in a real club.

:::tip[Try the season dashboard.]
In the new season folder, tap the leaderboard button to see the whole season in [Club stats](/info/club-stats/) — leaderboard, points race, money, and attendance.
:::

## What gets written

The simulation writes ordinary log entries — buy-ins, seating, level changes, knockouts, rebuys, add-ons, and any chip counts and hands you asked for — exactly as if the tournament had been run live. It respects your [rebuy period](/info/setup/#buy-in-and-features): simulated rebuys and add-ons all land inside it, or within the first four blind levels if you haven't set one. Everything flows into the [tournament log](/info/log/), [Stats & reporting](/info/stats/), and [payouts](/info/payouts/) just like a real game, so you can explore every feature with realistic data.
