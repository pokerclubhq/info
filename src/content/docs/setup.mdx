This guide walks you through creating and setting up a tournament in Poker Club HQ.

## Creating a tournament

To create a tournament, in the home screen, tap the + in the top right:

<Image src={setup00002a} alt="Home screen" width={320} />

In the 'Add new' menu, tap 'Tournament':

<Image src={setup00003a} alt="Add new item menu" width={320} />

If you already have other tournaments, a **Copy settings from** menu appears next. Choose **Default settings** for a fresh tournament, or pick an existing tournament (listed newest first) to reuse its setup.

<Image src={setup00003b} alt="The Copy settings from menu listing Default settings and the existing tournaments" width={320} />

:::tip[Copying settings]
Copying brings over the whole setup — chip set, blind levels, timing, antes, expected players, buy-in, add-on, and rebuy costs, bounty settings, table balancing, payouts, alerts, custom log types, points formula, stats columns, and screen customizations. It does **not** copy the tournament's title, players, log, chops, QR code, or cloud link — the new tournament starts empty.
:::

A new tournament is added and opened to its Settings screen:

<Image src={setup00004} alt="Settings screen" width={320} />

## Tournament settings

It's a good idea to give the tournament a title. A month and day works well — '01/17' for Jan 17:

<Image src={setup00005} alt="Tournament title" width={320} />

Next, update the number of expected players. This is important so that the blind structure includes enough levels. This example expects 18 players:

<Image src={setup00006a} alt="Expected players" width={320} />

## Adding chips

Then, tap to add chips:

:::note
Adding your chips is important so that the blinds structure only includes values that your chip set supports, and so that the 'Starting chips' and 'Initial big blind' are set to values that make sense.
:::

<Image src={setup00006b} alt="Add chips" width={320} />

The chips screen opens with the **Add chips** sheet already showing, because the tournament has no chips yet. (Coming back to a chip set that already has chips, tap the + in the top right to open the same sheet.)

<Image src={setup00007a} alt="Chips screen" width={320} />

Then tap the chip set that most closely matches the values of your chip set. Don't worry about colors or chip design, those can be changed later. In this example the closest match is 25, 100, 500, 1K, 5K:

<Image src={setup00008} alt="Chips menu" width={320} />

The chip set you chose will be created. You can tap a chip to edit its color and value, or to delete it. (If you're signed into an account, you can also upload custom images of your chips!) Once you're finished, tap the back button in the top left to go back to the tournament settings screen:

<Image src={setup00009a} alt="Chip set created" width={320} />

## Buy-in and features

Back in the Settings screen, tap the 'Buy-in cost' box and update its value — $20 here:

<Image src={setup00011} alt="Buy-in cost" width={320} />

The next section has some switches you can turn on for the features you want your tournament to support. This tournament supports addons, so tap the Addons switch to turn it on:

<Image src={setup00012a} alt="Addons switch" width={320} />

Turning on a feature will show related settings that can be adjusted:

<Image src={setup00013a} alt="Addon settings" width={320} />

If you expect around 6 players to purchase addons, update that setting to match:

<Image src={setup00014b} alt="Expected addons" width={320} />

**Rebuys/reentries** is the next switch, and it has one setting the others don't: **Rebuys end after**. Leave it on **No limit** and rebuys stay open all game. Pick a level instead and the rebuy period closes — "Level 3" means rebuys run through the third blind level *and* through the break that follows it, since that break is when rebuys and add-ons are usually taken. They close the moment the next blind level starts.

Levels are counted ignoring breaks, so the number matches what the timer shows. Only the first seven are listed, because rebuy periods are rarely longer than that — tap **Show more** if yours is.

Setting a period doesn't lock anything down. The app never refuses a late rebuy; it just notes on the [Rebuy sheet](/info/players/#rebuy--reentry) that the period has ended and leaves the call to you. On the timer, a [Rebuy countdown](/info/widgets/) widget shows the players how long they have left.

This tournament also supports bounties, so turn on the Bounties feature:

<Image src={setup00015b} alt="Bounties feature" width={320} />

The **Bounty chip cost** is charged in addition to the buy-in cost — it is not part of it. A player who buys in and takes a bounty chip pays the buy-in plus the bounty chip cost. Money paid for bounty chips is kept separate from the prize pool. See [Bounties](/info/bounties/) for the full walkthrough.

## Blind structure

The next section in Settings is related to the blind structure:

<Image src={setup00016} alt="Blind structure settings" width={320} />

To keep the tournament short, drag the 'Blind growth rate' slider to the far right — the further right, the faster the blinds grow:

<Image src={setup00017a} alt="Blind growth rate" width={320} />

Scrolling down you can see the entire blind structure that will be created:

<Image src={setup00018} alt="Full blind structure" width={320} />

This is a preview. It updates live as you change the settings above, but it isn't applied to the tournament until you tap **Save this blind structure** below it — and if the tournament already has a structure, you'll be asked to confirm before it's overwritten. **Show current blinds** opens the level editor if you'd rather build or adjust levels by hand. See [Blind structure](/info/blinds/).

## Saving and starting

The rest of the settings save as you edit them, so there's no separate save step for them. A brand-new tournament has no blind levels yet, so a 'Save' button appears in the top right — tap it to create the blind structure from your settings:

<Image src={setup00018a} alt="Save button" width={320} />

Once the tournament has a blind structure, you'll be taken to the tournament timer screen:

<Image src={setup00019} alt="Timer screen" width={320} />

:::note
If you created this tournament by copying settings from another one, it already has a blind structure — so no 'Save' button appears in the top right, and you can go to the timer whenever you're ready.
:::
