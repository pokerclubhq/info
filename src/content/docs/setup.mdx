This guide walks you through creating and setting up a tournament in Poker Club HQ.

## Creating a tournament

To create a tournament, in the home screen, tap the + in the top right:

<Image src={setup00002a} alt="Home screen" width={320} />

In the 'Add new' menu, tap 'Tournament':

<Image src={setup00003a} alt="Add new item menu" width={320} />

If you already have other tournaments, a **Copy settings from** menu appears next. Choose **Default settings** for a fresh tournament, or pick an existing tournament (listed newest first) to reuse its setup.

<Image src={setup00003b} alt="The Copy settings from menu listing Default settings and the existing tournaments" width={320} />

:::tip[Copying settings]
Copying brings over the whole setup — chip set, blind levels, timing, antes, expected players, buy-in, add-on, and rebuy costs, bounty settings, table balancing, payouts, alerts, custom log types, points formula, stats columns, and screen customizations. It does **not** copy the tournament's title, players, log, chops, QR code, or cloud link — the new tournament starts empty.
:::

A new tournament is added and opened to its Settings screen:

<Image src={setup00004} alt="Settings screen" width={320} />

## Tournament settings

It's a good idea to give the tournament a title. For example, I like to title it with the month and day of the tournament, '01/17' for Jan 17:

<Image src={setup00005} alt="Tournament title" width={320} />

Next, update the number of expected players. This is important so that the blinds structure includes enough levels. For example, I'm expecting 18 players:

<Image src={setup00006a} alt="Expected players" width={320} />

## Adding chips

Then, tap to add chips:

:::note
Adding your chips is important so that the blinds structure only includes values that your chip set supports, and so that the 'Starting chips' and 'Initial big blind' are set to values that make sense.
:::

<Image src={setup00006b} alt="Add chips" width={320} />

In the chips screen, tap the + in the top right:

<Image src={setup00007a} alt="Chips screen" width={320} />

And then tap the chip set that most closely matches the values of your chip set. Don't worry about colors or chip design, those can be changed later. For example, my chip set values most closely match 25, 100, 500, 1K, 5K, so that's what I tap:

<Image src={setup00008} alt="Chips menu" width={320} />

The chip set you chose will be created. You can tap a chip to edit its color and value, or to delete it. (If you're signed into an account, you can also upload custom images of your chips!) Once you're finished, tap the back button in the top left to go back to the tournament settings screen:

<Image src={setup00009a} alt="Chip set created" width={320} />

## Buy-in and features

Back in the Settings screen, tap the 'Buy-in cost' box and update its value. For example, the buy-in for my tournament is $20:

<Image src={setup00011} alt="Buy-in cost" width={320} />

The next section has some switches you can turn on for various features you want your tournament to support. My tournament will support addons, so I will tap the Addons switch to turn it on:

<Image src={setup00012a} alt="Addons switch" width={320} />

Turning on a feature will show related settings that can be adjusted:

<Image src={setup00013a} alt="Addon settings" width={320} />

I expect around 6 players to purchase addons, so I update that setting:

<Image src={setup00014b} alt="Expected addons" width={320} />

My tournament will also support bounties, so I turn on the Bounties feature:

<Image src={setup00015b} alt="Bounties feature" width={320} />

The **Bounty chip cost** is charged in addition to the buy-in cost — it is not part of it. A player who buys in and takes a bounty chip pays the buy-in plus the bounty chip cost. Money paid for bounty chips is kept separate from the prize pool. See [Bounties](/info/bounties/) for the full walkthrough.

## Blind structure

The next section in Settings is related to the blind structure:

<Image src={setup00016} alt="Blind structure settings" width={320} />

I want to keep my tournament to a shorter length, so I drag the 'Blind growth rate' slider to the far right:

<Image src={setup00017a} alt="Blind growth rate" width={320} />

Scrolling down you can see the entire blind structure that will be created:

<Image src={setup00018} alt="Full blind structure" width={320} />

## Saving and starting

When you're done editing settings, tap the 'Save' button in the top right:

<Image src={setup00018a} alt="Save button" width={320} />

After settings are saved, you'll be taken to the tournament timer screen:

<Image src={setup00019} alt="Timer screen" width={320} />
