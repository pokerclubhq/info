Need a hand running a tournament — a co-host on a second device, a partner working the players screen while you watch the clock? Add their email address to the tournament's **Permissions** list and they can run it alongside you, with every change syncing between you in real time.

This is different from [QR code sharing](/info/qr-sharing/), which gives anyone a read-only view without an account. Use Permissions when someone needs to *make changes*; use QR sharing when they just need to *watch*.

Co-editors can do just about everything you can — start and pause the timer, enter buy-ins and knockouts, move players between tables, edit the blind structure. Two things stay with you as the owner: managing this Permissions list, and deleting the tournament.

## Requirements

- Both of you must be signed into Poker Club HQ accounts.
- The tournament must be cloud-synced — it will be, once you're signed in.
- The co-editor's email address must be **verified**. Google and Apple sign-ins are verified automatically; accounts registered with a password need one extra step (see below).

## Granting access

1. Open the tournament's **Settings** screen.
2. Tap the **Permissions** button.

   <Image src={permissionsButton} alt="Tournament Settings screen with the Permissions button highlighted" width={320} />

3. The Permissions screen opens. The first time, it's empty — tap **Add email address**.

   <Image src={permissionsEmpty} alt="Empty Permissions screen with an Add email address button" width={320} />

4. Type the co-editor's email address and tap **Save**.

   <Image src={permissionsAddDialog} alt="Add email address dialog with an email entered" width={320} />

5. Repeat for anyone else. The list shows everyone who currently has edit access — tap the pencil to correct an address, or the trash icon to revoke it.

   <Image src={permissionsList} alt="Permissions screen showing three email addresses with edit and remove buttons" width={320} />

:::caution
It has to be the *right* address — the one on the co-editor's Profile screen. Capitalization doesn't matter, since the app stores addresses in lower case. But a typo doesn't produce an error; it just quietly leaves them with the read-only viewer. The surest way to avoid one is to have your co-editor copy their address out of the app and send it to you.
:::

## For the co-editor: your email address

Everything in this section is for the person being added.

Your address is on your Profile screen, and you can copy it straight from there:

1. Open the navigation drawer and tap **Profile**.
2. Tap the email address (or the copy icon beside it) to copy it to the clipboard.
3. Paste it into a text or email and send it to the tournament owner.

<Image src={profileNotVerified} alt="Profile screen showing the email address with a copy icon, an Email is not verified warning, and a Send verification email button" width={320} />

If you signed in with **Google** or **Apple**, your address is already verified — you're done here.

If you registered with **email and password**, your account starts out unverified, and the Profile screen shows an orange **Email is not verified** warning with a **Send verification email** button (above). Editing shared tournaments won't work until you clear it:

1. Tap **Send verification email**.
2. Open the email Poker Club HQ sends you and tap the link in it.
3. Come back to the app and open **Profile** again.

If your edits still don't stick a few minutes later, sign out and back in to refresh your credentials.

## Connecting to a shared tournament

Once the owner has added your verified address, you need a link to the tournament itself. Two ways to get one:

- **Scan the QR code.** Point your camera at the QR code on the owner's timer screen and tap the link that appears.
- **Tap a link they sent you.** The owner can tap the QR code on their timer to copy the link, then send it to you in a text or email.

Either way, the app opens the tournament and links you in as a co-editor. If the owner hasn't put a QR code on their timer yet, they'll need to create one first — see [QR code sharing](/info/qr-sharing/).

:::note
If you open the link and get a read-only timer instead of edit access, one of three things is off: your address isn't on the Permissions list, it doesn't match the one on your Profile screen, or it isn't verified yet.
:::
