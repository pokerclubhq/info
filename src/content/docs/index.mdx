Poker Club HQ runs your poker club or league end to end: blind structures, chip sets, registration, seating, and table balancing.

<Image src={timer02} alt="Timer screen" width={600} />

A customizable timer, QR sharing, and cloud sync keep every screen in step. Track buy-ins, rebuys, bounties, and chops, seat players by drag-and-drop with assisted balancing, and review it all with full undo/redo and exportable stats.

New in the latest beta: players connected to a shared tournament can now **[update their own profile and report their chips and hand](/info/player-updates/)** from their phone, a **[Chip leader](/info/timer-display/#chip-leader)** timer widget shows who's out in front, you can give your players **[photos](/info/player-directory/#player-photos)** and show everyone's face at their seat on the [Tables](/info/tables/#player-photos-or-names) screen, and create a tournament by **copying the settings** of an existing one.

Poker Club HQ is currently in beta, available on iPhones and iPads, Android phones and tablets, and on the web.

:::

## Feature highlights

### Club stats — season leaderboards, points races, money, and attendance
Compile stats across every completed tournament in a folder into a league-style season dashboard. See [Club stats](/info/club-stats/).

<Image src={clubStats} alt="Club stats points chart showing the top 10 players' cumulative points across a season" width={600} />

### Player photos — see every face at the table
Add photos to your players and the tables screen shows them right at their seats. See [Player photos](/info/player-directory/#player-photos).

<Image src={playerPhotos} alt="Two tables with every seated player shown as a photo at their seat" width={300} />

### Players keep their own profiles current
Connect players to a shared tournament and they can update their own photo and details — and report their chips and hand — right from their phone. See [Player profile updates](/info/player-updates/).

<Image src={playerUpdates00003} alt="The My player profile sheet with a Current game section for chips and hand and a profile summary below" width={300} />

### Bounty chip tracking
Track who bought a bounty chip, who holds whose bounty, and what each player has won — all kept separate from the prize pool. See [Bounties](/info/bounties/).

<Image src={bounties} alt="Bounties" width={300} />

### Chopping — ICM chop, chip chop, even chop, and custom chop
Settle a deal at the final table four different ways, with payouts recalculated for you. See [Payouts & chops](/info/payouts/).

<Image src={chop} alt="Chopping" width={300} />

### Comprehensive stats collection including custom player point calculation
Every finish, payout, and knockout is recorded, with a points formula you can write yourself. See [Stats & reporting](/info/stats/).

<Image src={points} alt="Player points" width={300} />

### Upload your custom chip images
Photograph your own chips and use them throughout the app. See [Chip sets](/info/chips/).

<Image src={chips} alt="Custom chips" width={500} />

### QR Code for real-time timer sharing
A QR Code can be added to your timer screen, letting anyone connect to a live view of the timer that they can't edit. See [QR code sharing](/info/qr-sharing/).

<Image src={qrcode} alt="QR code" width={600} />

### Tables can be named and the breakdown order customized
Give each table its own name and color, and control the order tables break down in. See [Managing tables](/info/tables/).

## The latest beta version supports the following features

* Creates high quality blind structures for tournaments of any size.
* Blind levels can also be created manually with complete flexibility.
* Create a new tournament by copying the settings of an existing one — structure, chips, timing, payouts, and more, without the players or log.
* Supports entry of chip sets with customizable values and colors, and ability to import your own chip images.
* Supports big blind antes, standard antes, or no antes.
* Includes a full screen timer that maximizes the size of the timer and blind values, and shows upcoming blinds and breaks, the average stack size per player, and estimated tournament end time — and can be customized to show just the information you want.
* Customizable alerts that play sounds or spoken announcements when conditions you define are met.
* A QR code can be added to the timer screen so anyone can follow a live view of it on their own device, without being able to edit it.
* Players connected through a shared tournament can update their own profile — photo, name, and contact details — and report their current chips and hand, which flow to the organizer's log and a Chip leader timer widget. See [Player profile updates](/info/player-updates/).
* Supports player and table management:
  * Add players to your tournament, import them from another tournament, or add a whole [Player Directory](/info/player-directory/#groups) group at once.
  * A flexible seating algorithm: seat a group with **Random order** and **Balance tables** and the app spreads them across tables where it can, and avoids putting them in adjacent seats when it can't.
  * A table view that shows all tables with the players and open seats at each table, and allows players to be drag-dropped between seats at a table or between tables.
  * Warns when tables are out of balance or can be consolidated, and helps with resolving each situation. Keeps track of how many times each player has been used for balancing tables and warns if they are picked again.
* Supports bounty chips, tracking who bought one, who holds whose bounty, and each player's bounty winnings — kept separate from the prize pool. A Bounties timer widget shows the top bounty winners, and Stats includes a total winnings column that combines payouts and bounty money.
* Keeps a detailed log of player buy-ins, addons, rebuys, reentries, knockouts, breaks, blinds, timer pauses and resumes, and the total play time for each player.
* Tracks the tournament's high hand — with an optional High hand timer widget — and lets you record player hands and chip counts right in the log with card pickers, plus a Hand evaluator in the main menu that ranks any Hold'em or Omaha hand and shows its win probability.
* A [hand timer](/info/hand-timer/) — a shot clock you can start from the main menu while the tournament clock is running, with a configurable number of seconds.
* A Stats screen that shows each player's place, payout, points, chips cost, chips bought, play time, players eliminated, and more — with the ability to choose which columns are shown and drag to reorder them. And supports exporting the shown columns as a CSV file for use in Excel or another spreadsheet app.
* A [Club stats](/info/club-stats/) dashboard that compiles a whole season of completed tournaments in a folder into leaderboards, points charts, and money and attendance stats.
* A payouts screen for calculating and customizing payout percentages and amounts, with support for ICM, chip, even, and custom chops.
* A Replay screen to visualize the entire tournament as players are seated, tables are balanced and consolidated, showing every step to the tournament to conclusion.
* A [Simulate](/info/simulate/) feature that instantly finishes a tournament with realistic hands, knockouts, and payouts — or generates a whole season of sample tournaments to explore Club stats.
* Organize tournaments into [folders](/info/folders/), and recover anything you delete from the [Trash](/info/trash/).
* An account feature to securely back up your tournaments to the cloud and share them between devices, with real-time syncing of changes.
* Share a tournament with co-editors who can help run it from their own devices.
