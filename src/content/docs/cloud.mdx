title: Cloud sync & accounts
description: How to create an account, sync tournaments across devices, and manage your profile in Poker Club HQ.
---

Poker Club HQ supports cloud backup and multi-device sync through optional user accounts. Your tournaments are stored locally by default, and signing in adds cloud synchronization on top.

## Creating an account

From the navigation drawer or settings, tap "Sign in" to access the sign-in screen. You can create an account using:

- **Google Sign-In**
- **Apple Sign-In**
- **Email and password**

After signing in, your tournaments automatically begin syncing to the cloud.

## How sync works

Cloud sync uses Firebase Firestore with offline-first architecture:

1. You make a change locally (add a player, start the timer, etc.)
2. The change is saved to the local cache immediately
3. When a network connection is available, the change syncs to the cloud within about 500 milliseconds
4. Other devices signed into the same account receive the change in real time
5. If you're offline, changes queue up and sync when connectivity returns

This means the app always works — even without an internet connection. Changes sync automatically when you're back online.

## Multi-device usage

Sign into the same account on multiple devices and your tournaments stay in sync. Changes made on one device appear on the others in real time. This is useful for:

- **Running a tournament from your phone** while displaying the timer on a tablet
- **Switching devices** mid-tournament without losing any data
- **Backing up** your tournaments to the cloud automatically

## Clock synchronization

To keep data consistent across devices, Poker Club HQ uses NTP (Network Time Protocol) to verify that device clocks are synchronized. If a device's clock drifts more than 10 seconds from the actual time, sync is temporarily disabled to prevent timestamp conflicts. Sync resumes automatically once the clock is back in range.

:::note
If you see a clock drift warning, check your device's date and time settings and make sure "Set automatically" is turned on.
:::

## Profile management

Tap your profile in the navigation drawer (or go to the Profile screen) to:

- **Update your display name** and profile photo
- **Manage sign-in methods** — Link or unlink Google and Apple accounts to the same profile
- **View account details** — Email address and verification status

## Account deletion

You can permanently delete your account from the Profile screen. When you delete your account:

1. Cloud syncing pauses immediately
2. A confirmation dialog appears
3. Your Firebase account and all cloud data are removed
4. Local copies of your tournaments remain on your device but no longer sync

:::note
Account deletion is permanent. Your cloud tournament data cannot be recovered after deletion, but local copies on your device are preserved.
:::
