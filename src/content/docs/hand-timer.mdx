---
title: Hand timer
description: How to use the hand timer — a shot clock for putting a time limit on a single hand — in Poker Club HQ.
---

The hand timer is a shot clock. When a player is taking too long, start it and a countdown appears on screen for everyone to see. It's separate from the tournament clock, which keeps running the whole time.

## Starting it

Open the tournament's menu (the button in the top right of the timer screen) and tap the **Hand timer** button in the top row of the drawer. The drawer closes and the countdown appears.

:::note
The button only shows up while the tournament clock is running. If the tournament is paused, there's nothing to time a hand against.
:::

## The countdown

The hand timer is a translucent circle that floats above whatever screen you're on, so it stays visible while you move between the timer, players, and tables.

- The number in the middle is the seconds remaining, counting down. Past a minute it reads as minutes and seconds.
- The spade behind it rotates as the seconds tick by, so the clock is readable from across the room.
- **Drag the circle** anywhere on screen. It reappears in the same spot next time.
- Tap **Cancel** at the bottom of the circle to stop early.

When the countdown reaches zero the circle disappears.

## Sounds

Three [alerts](/info/alerts/) come switched on for the hand timer, so you don't have to watch the screen:

- **Hand timer start** — a drum fill when you start it
- **Hand timer countdown** — the last ten seconds spoken aloud, one by one
- **Hand timer end** — an accent when time is up

They're ordinary alerts, so you can change the sounds, rewrite them, or turn them off on the tournament's Alerts screen.

:::note
Like all alerts, these keep playing on every screen of the tournament, so you still hear the countdown after moving to Players or Tables.
:::

## Changing the number of seconds

The hand timer starts at 30 seconds. To change it, **tap the circle while it's running**: the countdown stops and a **Hand timer seconds** box opens. Enter anything from 1 second to 10 minutes and the timer restarts with the new value.

That number becomes the new default, so the next hand you time uses it too. The setting lives on the device and applies to every tournament on it.

## Stopping it

Any of these work:

- Tap **Cancel** on the circle
- Reopen the tournament menu and tap **Hand timer** again
- Let it run out
